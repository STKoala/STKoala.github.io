I’ve recently decided to take this blog seriously.

For me, blogging is not just about “outputting content”; it’s more like building a long‑term thinking system:

1. Turning scattered thoughts into structured writing;
2. Transforming experiments, pitfalls, and experiences into reusable knowledge;
3. Leaving a traceable record of growth for my future self.

Going forward, I plan to focus on three main types of content:

- **Research Notes**: time series foundation models, knowledge distillation, and related topics;
- **Development Learning**: documenting what I learn during development;
- **AI Infra**: recording and sharing knowledge about AI infrastructure.

If you’re working in similar areas, feel free to reach out.
